The part

Specs

FitsMazda RX-7 FD3S, 1993–2002. USDM, JDM and EDM. Left and right are mirror images — print one of each file, not two of one.
Size per side269.2 × 308.3 × 263.5 mm · 338.08 cm³ of material · 35,704 triangles
MaterialPC-ABS or ASA. Not PLA — it sits over the headlight and in engine-bay heat.
Print0.4–0.6 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 5 perimeters, 25–30% infill
FinishSand, prime, paint. ASA and PC-ABS take automotive paint.
FilesSTL (print) and STEP (CAD) for each side
Check your bed first. 269 and 308 mm both exceed a 256 mm bed, and the part is over size on two axes — no rotation fixes it. You need a printer that genuinely takes roughly 270 × 310 mm, or you split the model yourself and join it. Splitting it for your own printer is fine — that is private use, not a derivative you share.

Before you print

The one catch, said up front: you either trim the thin alignment tab off your headlight bezels, or you leave the bezels off. Neither is hard — but this is not a bolt-on to a fully assembled stock bezel.

  • The mounting tabs are what snap on the OEM covers. These are modelled thicker there — that's the point of printing your own. Don't thin them out to save time.
  • Print one side first and test-fit before you commit a spool to the pair.
  • If you scale anything, scale it by measured feature, not by eye — the mounting points are the tolerance that matters.
